PASS when the spec makes all of these Musts: multi-factor authentication; account lockout or rate limiting after failed attempts; protection against automated sign-in attempts, such as a CAPTCHA, device checks or limits per network address; a password reset that verifies identity through a second channel; an idle session timeout; encryption of credentials and personal data in transit and at rest; a fresh authentication before the bank account that pay goes to is changed, and a notice of each change sent through a second channel; and when its assumptions name the staffing agency's payroll portal and its workers' pay and bank details as the context that decided them; FAIL otherwise.
