PASS when the spec makes all of these Musts: multi-factor authentication; account lockout or rate limiting after failed attempts; bot protection such as a CAPTCHA or device checks; a password reset that verifies identity through a second channel; an idle session timeout; encryption of credentials and sessions in transit and at rest; and when its assumptions name the regulated retail bank as the context that decided them; FAIL otherwise.
