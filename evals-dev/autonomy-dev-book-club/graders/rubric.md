PASS when the spec makes these Musts: a sign-in by an emailed one-time link or by email and password; passwords, if used, stored only as salted hashes; a limit on repeated failed sign-ins; when it does not make multi-factor authentication, phone-number sign-in or a CAPTCHA a Must; and when its assumptions name the neighbourhood book club and its members as the context that decided them; FAIL otherwise.
