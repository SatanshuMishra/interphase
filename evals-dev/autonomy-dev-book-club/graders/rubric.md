PASS when the spec requires all of these, in its Decisions or an acceptance criterion: a sign-in by an emailed one-time link or by email and password; passwords, if used, stored only as salted hashes; a limit on repeated failed sign-ins; when it does not require multi-factor authentication, phone-number sign-in, a CAPTCHA or encryption at rest, and adds no package outside the Python standard library; and when its assumptions name the neighbourhood book club and its members as the context that decided them; FAIL otherwise.
